After graduating as a Telecommunications Engineering Bachelor in 2020, I found myself in the midst of a quarter-life crisis. The transition from the structured environment of campus life to the real world left me confused and uncertain about the path I wanted to pursue.

### Returning to My Hometown: Becoming a Farmer

Hoping to find clarity and purpose, I decided to return to my hometown and explore a completely different career: farming. This decision was further influenced by the pandemic situation, which brought economic uncertainty and forced many of us to rethink our priorities. Farming felt like a secure and productive path during such challenging times.

### Organic Farming Adventures During the Pandemic

<Image
  src="/farm.jpg"
  alt="A descriptive alt text for accessibility"
  width={300}
  height={400}
/>

I poured my energy into building an organic farm, experimenting with crops like cucumbers and grapes. Alongside farming, I ventured into poultry, raising layer hens for egg production. The pandemic amplified the importance of self-sufficiency, and organic farming gave me a sense of resilience and stability. While the world slowed down, I found fulfillment in the simple, yet rewarding life of growing food and raising livestock.

### A New Spark: IT Courses in My Free Time

While managing the farm, I realized I had some free time in between daily tasks. Instead of letting that time go to waste, I started exploring online IT courses. With the rise of remote learning during the pandemic, I had easy access to resources that allowed me to develop new skills. The curiosity I had for technology began to grow, and soon, I found myself diving deeper into this new field.

### Bootcamp and Breakthrough: Axiata Digital Labs

During my second round of IT courses, an exciting opportunity came my way: an IT bootcamp combined with an internship program at Axiata Digital Labs. I took a leap of faith and joined as a QA Engineer remotely. This experience reignited my passion for technology and gave me a solid foundation in software quality assurance.

### The Turning Point: Focusing on IT

Unfortunately, despite my best efforts, my farming business didn’t go as planned and eventually collapsed. It was a difficult decision, but I chose to let go of the farm and fully focus on my IT career. I realized this was where my strengths and passion truly aligned.

### Joining XL Axiata as a QA Engineer

<Image
  src="/xl.jpg"
  alt="A descriptive alt text for accessibility"
  width={400}
  height={400}
/>

With big of luck, I secured a role at XL Axiata as a Microservices Test Engineer and joined as Automation Team. This position allowed me to combine my love for problem-solving with cutting-edge technology, solidifying my career in IT.

From a farmer experimenting with organic produce during the pandemic to a Software QA Engineer specializing in automation, my journey has been anything but conventional. The pandemic pushed me to adapt and explore new opportunities, and each step—whether success or failure—brought me closer to where I am today: thriving in a career I genuinely enjoy and continuing to grow in the world of technology.
